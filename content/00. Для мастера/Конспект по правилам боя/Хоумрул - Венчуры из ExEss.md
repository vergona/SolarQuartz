## Travel

Travel ventures are ventures that involve travel across the environment in some way, shape, or form.

### Chase/Footrace

A Chase/Footrace is a venture used for describing some sort of test of speed. The intervals involved are very short.

Chases have a special rule compared to other gambits. When a chase first starts all opponents make their first opposed roll against each other, with the winner of this roll being counted as the one in the leader. The leader may then choose before selecting any charms and rolling their action whenever they wish to roll against the difficulty of one of the selected obstacles or roll against the other characters. Whoever gathers the most threshold successes on the roll is considered to be the new leader. This process repeats until the character involved in the chase clears all obstacles, in which case they win the venture. If two characters tie, then the winner is decided by an opposed roll on the next round.

Characters who have a significant edge in speed, such as a horse racing against a man or a Solar activating Racing Hare Method to leave an Earth caste Immaculate in the dust, do not need to bother using this venture. Those who use mounts add a single automatic success to all rolls if their speed rating is higher than that of the opponent.

Note: This venture replaces the withdraw action in its entirety. To declare this venture in combat, then all one has to do is move to medium range and take a movement action away from all active opponents. If at least one opponent wishes to contest then this venture begins.

- Prerequisites: None
- Terminus: None
- Time Scale: One Round
- Common Dice Pool: Dexterity + Athletics (on foot), Wits+Ride (Horseback/vehicle)
- Base Obstacles: 5

**Sample Obstacles:**

- Variable

- Deprivation/Fatigue (7 - Stamina, minimum 3). If this applies to an animal, use half of their (Endure Hardship) pool in place of Stamina, rounded up.
- Getting Attacked (Opposed, Storyteller may rule attack resolves as a normal attack)
- Speed Rating of a mount (if animal has a speed of +3 or higher, this can include charm boosted values)

- Difficulty 3

- Visibility is considered poor, unable to see beyond medium range
- Terrain is problematic; loose sand/snow, tall grass, ankle deep water, slick roads, leaping rooftop to rooftop, or loose cobblestones
- Weather is considered hazardous. Heavy rainstorm, heavy winds that slow those racing against it, or the blistering daytime sun of Gem

- Difficulty 5

- Visibility is considered very poor, unable to see beyond short range
- Terrain is considered dangerous or significantly slows one down; a crowd of people, knee deep water, icy roads, or a rickety bridge.
- Weather is considered very hazardous. Typhoon level rainstorms or wind capable of supporting one’s weight.
- Opponent has Fleet of Foot Merit

- Difficulty 7

- Near or no visibility, unable to see beyond close range
- Terrain is considered extremely dangerous or difficult to traverse; quicksand, water up to one’s chest, near frictionless surface, racing across a tightrope.
- Weather is extremely hazardous; high speed water currents capable of easily carrying people water, winds that can hurl a man into the air.

**Sample Advantages:**
 Variable

- Force an opponent to content with single additional obstacle (+1 difficulty per 2 successes, maximum 5)
- Enhance the difficulty of a currently existing obstacle (+1 difficulty per 2 successes, maximum 7)

- 3-4 successes

- Dazzle crowd of onlookers, allowing an automatically successful inspire action against all onlookers (3)
- Make an attack against an opponent without flurry penalties (4)

- 5+ successes

- Automatically gain surprise against opponent if combat follows after chase (5)

- Sample Consequences: death/destruction of mount/vehicle, becoming lost, collateral damage to bystanders/environment, fatigue penalties to all actions, being forced to engage in combat again.
